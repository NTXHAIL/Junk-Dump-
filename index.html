How it works

Two ways to rent. Zero stress.

  1. 1

    Get a quote

    Call, text or send a quote request. Choose a direct trailer rental or a driveway trailer drop, and tell us when you need it.

  2. 2

    Get the trailer

    Direct rental: the trailer is yours for the rental period. We'll go over pickup and towing when we confirm your quote. Driveway drop: we park it right where you want it.

  3. 3

    You load, we haul

    Fill it at your own pace. When you're done, we haul it away and take care of the dumping. For a direct rental, we'll confirm how the trailer comes back when we confirm your quote.

Loading rules

What can go in it?

Not sure about an item? Call or text before you load it.

✓ Yes, toss it

  • Storm debris: downed limbs & branches
  • Water-damaged drywall, carpet & padding
  • Damaged fencing, wood & cabinets
  • Roofing shingles (heavy, so they count fast toward your 1 ton)
  • Furniture: couches, chairs, tables, mattresses
  • Household junk, boxes & clutter
  • Flooring & small remodel debris

✕ No, please keep out

  • Hazardous chemicals, pesticides & pool chemicals
  • Paint, stains & solvents (liquid)
  • Tires
  • Batteries (car and household)
  • Motor oil, gasoline, generator fuel & other fuels
  • Propane tanks & pressurized cylinders
  • Appliances with refrigerant (fridges, A/C units)
  • Asbestos, medical waste & anything that's on fire or leaking

⚖️ Heads-up on weight: shingles, concrete, dirt and wet storm debris get heavy fast. Each rental includes 1 ton (2,000 lb); overweight loads are charged per our quoted rate, and loads can't exceed the trailer's 7K–10K GVWR rating.

FAQ

Good questions

What's the difference between a direct rental and a driveway drop?

With a driveway drop, we bring the trailer to you, you load it, and we haul it away. A direct rental means you rent the trailer for your own project. We offer daily and weekly rentals, and a delivery fee may apply depending on your location. We'll confirm pickup, towing, and return details with your quote.

Can you help with hurricane or storm debris?

Yes. We offer driveway trailer drop-offs for storm clean-up across the Florida Panhandle, from Destin to Panama City Beach: we drop the trailer, you load the debris, and we haul it away. After a big storm, timing depends on road conditions, safety and demand, so we can't promise a specific arrival time. Request a quote as early as you can, or call or text to ask what timing looks like.

What area do you serve?

We serve the Florida Panhandle from Destin to Panama City Beach, including the 30A corridor in between. Just outside that stretch? Call or text us and ask.

Where can you put the trailer?

Most customers have it placed on a driveway. If you want it on the street, check with your city, county or HOA first, since some require a permit. Let us know about tight spots, gates, low branches or storm debris in the way when you request your quote.

How much does it cost?

We offer daily and weekly rentals. Pricing depends on the trailer, your dates and your location, so get a quote or message us for details. You can also join the waiting list if we're booked up. Every option includes 1 ton (2,000 lb) of weight. For extra-day and prohibited-item fees, get a quote or message us.

Is there a delivery fee?

For direct trailer rentals, the delivery fee depends on your location. Send your address or ZIP with a quote request, or call or text us, and we'll let you know.

How much weight is included?

Each rental includes 1 ton (2,000 lb). Loads over that are charged per our quoted rate. Loads can't exceed the trailer's rated capacity (7K–10K GVWR), and an overloaded trailer has to be lightened before we can haul it. Heads-up: shingles, concrete, dirt and wet storm debris get heavy fast, so ask us if you're unsure.

How long can I keep it?

As long as you need it. We offer daily and weekly rentals. Need more time? Call or text and ask.

Do I need to be home for drop-off or pickup?

Not usually. As long as the spot is clear and we know where to place it, we can drop off and pick up while you're out.

What if something on the "no" list ends up in the trailer?

We may need to remove it before dumping, and extra fees can apply. Call or text for details. When in doubt, ask us first and we're happy to help you figure out where it can go.

How do I pay?

Call or text us and we'll tell you how to pay.
